# Nodio 0.6.5

**Release date:** June 2026

The main navigation is more compact by default: **List view** lives in the top bar, and the second **World canvas / List / Board** strip under the header is off. You can change both anytime under **App Settings → Navigation**.

Canvas and Kanban are hardened for the **interactive tutorial**: panning, **group selection**, and moving Kanban cards no longer cause freezes or console errors.

---

## Improvements

- **Navigation — compact default:** Fresh installs and reset settings start with **list view** in the **main bar** and **without** the canvas sub-navigation — more vertical space for your workspace. Prefer the segment strip under the header? Turn **Show canvas sub-navigation** back on; **list view** and the switcher can be active at the same time.
- **Navigation — configurable:** Under **App Settings → Navigation**, **Show canvas sub-navigation** and **List view** (main bar) are separate toggles. Hiding the sub-navigation automatically enables list view in the main bar. Settings persist in **Nodio.ini** **`[Navigation]`** (`ShowCanvasSubnav`, `ShowListView`). **Settings** help (DE/EN) updated.
- **Interactive tutorial — extended:** The canvas quest tutorial now has **ten steps**: step 10 covers **right-click** → **Properties** (note or group). After completion, the **discover panel** suggests interactive follow-up tutorials — first **Kanban workflow** (five steps on your current project: create board, assign, dates, open board, move card). Kanban prerequisite: at least one note in the project.
- **Editor — toolbar (industry-standard layout):** The **main toolbar** uses **two rows** — top **document & insert** (undo, **Insert** menu, link, Nodio actions), bottom **formatting** (paragraph style dropdown, bold/italic/underline/strikethrough, lists, blockquote, clear formatting). **Selected text** shows a **compact quick-format bar** (bubble). The **right-click menu** is limited to context (image, mention, link) and clipboard — no full toolbar copy. **Editor — Edit** help and onboarding copy updated.
- **Workspace — consistent buttons:** On **My projects**, **New project**, and the **template** dialog, primary actions (e.g. **New project**, **Create project**) now match **Settings** and modals: flat theme primary instead of gradient, subtle shadow, consistent corners. Secondary actions (open, cancel) and the **project properties** modal follow the same pattern.
- **Kanban quest — six steps:** The interactive **Planning & structure** tutorial now covers **assign**, **dates**, **board**, **move card**, **timeline tab**, and **predecessor/successor** links. An existing Kanban board is a **prerequisite** (no longer step 1) — dialog hint if no board exists yet.
- **Help → Start interactively:** Starting the Kanban tutorial from the **help window** switches to the **main window** on the canvas and **closes help** — the quest card appears as expected.
- **Kanban quest — two work packages:** Step 1 requires **two board assignments**, step 2 **dates on two notes** — prerequisite at least **two notes** in the project. Step 6 (dependency) completes only when **predecessor and successor** on the edge each have a date.
- **Help Center — layout:** The help window uses the **custom title bar** (like changelog, without graph search). **Search** sits to the right of the heading; the **tab bar** is narrower instead of full width. On the right: **context help** in two lines (**Help for current view** + topic name) — stays on the **app area** you opened help from when you browse other topics; click returns to that topic. Without app context: **No route context** hint. Under **Shortcuts**: **list** and **keyboard map** views (view only).
- **Navigation — adapts to window width:** When the top **main bar** does not have enough room for text labels next to icons, Nodio automatically switches to **icons only** (with tooltips on hover) instead of a horizontal scrollbar under the navigation. When you widen the window, labels return once there is enough space. The **Main navigation: icons only** setting under **App Settings → Navigation** is unchanged and still takes precedence.
- **Help tooltips — faster to appear:** Short hints in pill style now show by default after **about 0.75 s** instead of 1.5 s — less waiting when hovering help buttons and unlabeled icons. Existing installs are **migrated once** on update to the new default; delay remains adjustable under **App Settings → Design** (0 s = immediate). The **editor toolbar** (formatting, insert, paragraph style) uses the same delay — no longer instant on hover.
- **Editor — metadata in sidebar:** In **edit mode**, **main title**, **summary**, and **aliases** live in the metadata column under **Identity** — the main writing area shows only the note body (more room to write). **Summary** can pop out to a larger floating window, like **connections**. In **read mode**, the title stays at the top; the summary is collapsible. **Editor — Edit** help (DE/EN) updated.
- **Canvas — edit connection types faster:** In connection dialogs (**Edit connection**, **Choose relation type**, **body connection**), **Create custom connection types** jumps straight to **Project dashboard → Templates & workflow → Connection types**. **Custom** relation types show a **pencil** on the right — click to open the same place with **that type pre-selected**. **World canvas** help (DE/EN) updated.
- **Editor — pop-out windows side by side:** When **summary** and **connections** are both popped out or minimized, the floating panels or restore tabs appear **side by side** instead of stacked — shared tray at the bottom left of the editor.
- **Editor — writing area:** The edit area uses the **full available height** when you open a note and has only **one scrollbar** — no more double scrollbars between the editor and the main column.
- **Kanban — Manage board:** **Rename board**, **Delete board**, and **Configure columns** are merged into **Manage board** — one wide dialog for board name, columns (add, reorder, appearance), **archived columns** (restore or delete permanently), **column import** from other boards in the project, and board deletion in the danger zone. **Kanban board** help (DE/EN) and **First board** onboarding tour updated.
- **Kanban — Manage board (column UX):** Columns in the dialog are **collapsed by default** (expand via chevron on the left); **status vs. bucket** explanation; add status and bucket columns **side by side**.
- **Interactive journal tour:** New **Journal** quest tutorial (6 steps) in Help Center and Discover panel — open hub, create a daily entry, write, set mood, weekly view, and search. Journal is auto-enabled for the project on start when needed. The Kanban button is now labeled **Start project management tour**.
- **Interactive views tour:** New **Views** quest tutorial (7 steps) in Help Center and Discover panel — open views, create a folder, save three canvas snapshots (including expand/collapse groups), switch between views, and start a folder slideshow. Prerequisite: at least **two notes** and **one group** in the project. Spotlight tour **First views** remains available in Help Center.
- **Achievements — subtle notification:** Unlocked achievements appear as a **toast in the bottom-right corner** instead of a full-screen overlay — with icon, confetti, and a link to **View all achievements**, without interrupting your workflow. Dismiss via X, Escape, or after about 5 seconds.
- **Context menus — unified:** Right-click menus on the **world canvas** (note, group, canvas object, map pin) and on the **Kanban board** share the same menu shell — consistent positioning, Escape, and click-outside dismiss. Menus grow with their content; unnecessary scrollbars are avoided when there is enough screen space.
- **Kanban — leaner context menu:** **Status** and **bucket column** in the board context menu are **collapsed by default** (like **nest under** for notes/groups) — the current value is shown on the row; expand to choose another option.
- **Tutorial — scrollable discover panel:** After the canvas basics tutorial, the **discover panel** stays at the bottom left — header and **Done** stay pinned; content scrolls when space is tight instead of overflowing off screen.
- **Tutorial — discover panel redesigned:** **Interactive tutorials** (Kanban, Journal, Views) start as three colored buttons side by side. Under **Spotlight tours**, **Explore in Help Center** opens the Help tutorials tab — instead of many individual cards in the panel. Labels clearly separate quest tutorials from spotlight tours.
- **Canvas Time-Lapse — layer by layer:** The step-size dropdown adds **Layer by Layer** — each logged change (note, group, connection, …) becomes its own frame. Ideal when many steps happened in the same time window (e.g. via MCP).
- **Canvas Time-Lapse — MP4 export:** Export produces **MP4/H.264** instead of WebM. In the player bar you choose **quality** (Low/Medium/High). Videos are **seekable** in standard players and look sharper than before.
- **Export branding — unified:** The same **“Created with Nodio”** row appears bottom-right on **canvas/views PNG**, in the **PDF footer**, **Web viewer**, and **Time-Lapse MP4**. Branding is always on for end users; **disable** only in the **Dev Workbench** for **PNG** and **Time-Lapse** export (PDF and Web viewer always include branding).
- **App settings — Simple and Advanced:** In the sidebar you choose **Simple** or **Advanced**. All categories (Design, AI, MCP, Performance, …) stay visible; in simple mode technical options are hidden (e.g. MCP port, LOD, Ghost View, Whisper GPU). The switch **saves immediately** — no **Apply settings** step. New installs default to **Simple**.
- **Setup wizard — leaner:** First-run setup is now **four steps** (language, usage profile, look and feel, finish). Sound, shortcuts, updates, and performance use sensible defaults and remain under **App settings**.
- **Setup wizard — usage profiles:** Five profiles (**Balanced**, **Knowledge & notes**, **Worldbuilding & fiction**, **Software development**, **Game development**) control visible areas, startup behavior, and a matching **color scheme**. Profile selection is the central step.
- **Chrome color packs — expanded:** Ten color schemes for the app chrome — new packs include **Slate**, **Amber**, **Amethyst**, **Sand**, **Crimson**, and **Ink** (plus Default, Ocean, Rose, Forest). Pickable in setup and under **App settings → Design**.
- **Setup wizard — window size:** The wizard window uses about **65 % width** and **50 % height** of the monitor work area (at least **720×720 px**, capped at 90 % / 85 %) and opens centered — profile cards are no longer cut off.
- **Privacy — expanded:** Under **App settings → Privacy** you now get **At a glance** (plain-language summary), fuller optional network disclosures (updates/roadmap, bug assistant, Whisper download, ElevenLabs read-aloud, web viewer export, help videos), **cross-links** to related settings, **data paths** with Explorer buttons for app configuration and nodio.ini, a contact note pointing to nodio.dev, and a **version stamp**.
- **AI & MCP — canvas spacing:** The embedded **AI assistant** and **MCP server** now instruct models to plan **spacing** when placing notes, groups, and canvas objects — existing positions are read first, coordinates set explicitly. Without coordinates, new items default to (0,0) and overlap.
- **ElevenLabs — settings in AI assistant:** **API key**, **voice picker**, and **custom voice ID** now live entirely under **App settings → AI assistant** (**Advanced** mode → **Assistant voice (read aloud)**). The **Assistant & speech** panel in the project dashboard is removed. The global ElevenLabs key follows the same pattern as other API keys; custom voice ID remains per project. Legacy per-project keys can be cleared; links from focus assistant and legacy URL `?mainTab=aiAssistant` route to app settings.
- **App settings — automatic saving:** Changes now save **instantly** as you edit them — no more clicking **Apply settings**. A small status below the content shows **Saving…**, **Saved automatically**, or an error hint; the next change automatically triggers a retry.
- **App settings — new Project section:** **Appearance**, **Backups**, **Files**, and **Database** for the active project now live centrally under **App settings → Project** — the same features as before on the project dashboard, just in one place. The dashboard's **Settings** tab links there via a button; without an open project, the section shows a hint with a link to the project list.
- **Dictation hotwords — unified:** App-wide and project-scoped replacement rules now appear together under **App settings → AI assistant → Dictation hotwords**. Per rule, a **dropdown** lets you choose **App-wide** or **This project only** — switching moves the rule; if the target scope already has a rule for the same word, Nodio asks before overwriting it. **Search** and **sort by word** help with many entries.
- **Dictation — hotwords:** After Whisper transcription you can define **word → replacement** rules (e.g. “Zet” → “Z”). **App settings → AI assistant → Dictation hotwords** applies workspace-wide; **Project dashboard → Settings → Dictation hotwords (project)** adds project-specific terms and **overrides** global rules when the word matches. Applies in **live preview** and the final result (focus orb, bug reporter). An **empty replacement** removes the matched word. Help **AI assistant** (DE/EN) updated.

## Bug fixes

- **Kanban — Manage board:** Collapsing a column showed **?** instead of a chevron — `expandLess` is not in the icon set; expand/collapse now uses `expandMore` with rotation (same as other accordions in the app).
- **Help — Tutorials:** Starting an interactive quest tutorial showed **?** instead of an icon — `flag` was not registered in the icon set; now `school`.

- **Canvas — group selection:** **Group selection** with a mixed multi-select (note, canvas object, note inside a group) no longer freezes the app. Causes included repeated graph reloads and DOM mutations from the multi-select overlay.
- **Canvas — update loop:** Loading and panning the canvas (including early tutorial steps) no longer triggers a **Maximum update depth** warning. The multi-select toolbar no longer reacts to every pan frame without an active selection; selection is cleared via the official XYFlow selection path.
- **Kanban — move card:** Dragging a card between columns no longer throws `setNodes is not defined` (optimistic update via the project graph store).
- **Editor — underline:** Removed duplicate TipTap `underline` extension (console warning); underline is provided via StarterKit.
- **Canvas — keyboard shortcuts:** Fixed missing `useKeyboardShortcutsStore` import in `WorldFlowCanvasInner` — canvas no longer crashes on open.
- **Tutorial — start from Help:** **Start interactively** in the help pop-out only navigated inside the help window; the tutorial seemed not to start. Now focuses the main window + canvas route (same pattern as spotlight tours).
- **Graph search — title substring:** Searching for `test` reliably finds notes like **MyTest** again (FTS path adds LIKE fallback for title/summary/content).
- **Schedule — cycles:** Opposing predecessor/successor edges (A↔B) are reported as a cycle after save.
- **Canvas Time-Lapse — navigation:** The full-screen player covered the **main navigation** with no clear way back. The player now stacks correctly, with **Back to Canvas** and a draggable title bar.
- **Canvas Time-Lapse — export:** Exported videos sometimes showed **0 seconds** duration or wrong framing. Each step is now held for a duration tied to **player speed**; framing follows canvas content.
- **Canvas Time-Lapse — fidelity:** Replay and MP4 export now use the same card size, colors, content/preview images, group frames, and canvas graphics as the live canvas (positions still from the audit log).
- **Canvas Time-Lapse — positions:** After multi-select drags, group moves, and moved canvas graphics, replay positions match the live canvas again.
- **Canvas Time-Lapse — group children:** Notes inside a group now move with the group in replay — even when individual child moves are not written to the audit log.
- **Canvas — sub-canvas context menu:** **Open sub-canvas** shows reliably in the right-click menu when the Dev Workbench bypass for sub-canvas is enabled (double-click and breadcrumb unchanged).
- **Canvas — sub-canvas breadcrumb:** The breadcrumb bar at the top of a nested canvas is **horizontally centered** instead of left-aligned.
- **Canvas — type change:** When you change a note type in the editor (e.g. to **canvas**) and return to the world canvas, cards and the context menu reflect the update immediately — no need to reopen the project.
- **Canvas — sub-canvas: new items landed at root:** Notes, groups, and canvas objects (shapes, text, images, icons) created while inside an open sub-canvas used to always appear in the **root canvas** instead of the currently open sub-canvas — affected the toolbar buttons **New node**/**New group**, inserting objects, **Group from selection**, the new-group keyboard shortcut, and the hint button on an empty canvas. All of these paths now reliably respect the currently open sub-canvas.
